# Code of conduct

We commit to a harassment-free and respectful project environment. Discuss methods, evidence, and
code without making assumptions about another person's body, health, disability, gender, diet,
training level, or access to care.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, publishing another person's
private data, sexualized content, threats, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, remove,
or reject contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub reporting
features. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
