# Data card

## Scope

CutSignal does not bundle a real health cohort, a third-party food database, or a personal export.
The packaged demo, executable cases, and known-truth evaluation are deterministic synthetic
fixtures. They verify software and declared behavior; they are not representative samples,
external validation, or evidence about an individual.

## Normalized records

| Record | Field group | Canonical unit / type | Missing and duplicate behavior | Main risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DailyObservation` | date, weight, intake, active energy, steps, sleep, training time, resting HR, HRV | ISO date; kg; kcal/day; count/day; hours; minutes; bpm; ms | Missing values remain null; absent calendar dates are explicit model gaps; generic duplicate dates use median weight, sums for additive daily fields, and means for rate/duration fields | Self-report bias, device overlap, clock/time-zone shifts, fluid noise, nonrandom missingness |
| `StrengthSet` | date, exercise, load, reps, RPE, set type, body weight, duration | ISO date; kg; count; 1–10 RPE; seconds | Sets remain separate; warm-ups and high-rep sets are retained but excluded from eligible e1RM trends | Exercise-name drift, technique/range changes, equipment differences, RPE subjectivity |
| `FoodItem` | serving, energy, macro/micronutrients, price, time, preferences, tags/allergens, uncertainty | g; kcal; g; mg or declared micronutrient unit; local currency units; minutes; probabilities/scores | Items remain separate by `food_id`; absent nutrients are not silently imputed by the optimizer | Label error, serving mismatch, price/availability drift, crowd-data error, incomplete allergen metadata |
| `DietConstraints` | days, nutrition bounds, budget/time, variety and eligibility rules | 1–14 days and units matching `FoodItem` | Unknown fields and impossible ranges are rejected | User-supplied targets may be inappropriate despite mathematical feasibility |

Pydantic range constraints are documented in `src/cutsignal/models.py`, which is the executable
schema. This table explains intent and is not a substitute for that contract.

## Lineage

```text
user export or optional API response
  -> bounded adapter and unit conversion
  -> validated normalized model + SourceRef
  -> analysis/optimization configuration
  -> typed result + run receipt
  -> private-detail or structurally minimized share-safe report
```

Every normalized record can carry provider, record type, source ID, device, original unit, and
confidence. Adapters must document aggregation/reconciliation rules. Reports do not prove that an
upstream source was complete or correct.

## Source, license, and version boundaries

- User exports remain user-controlled and are not committed.
- USDA FoodData Central is optional and not mirrored. The normalizer uses explicit nutrient IDs and
  units; kJ is converted to kcal and mass units are normalized before optimization.
- Open Food Facts is optional, crowdsourced, and subject to ODbL/content licenses. Its confidence is
  lower and local price/allergen/availability claims remain the user's responsibility.
- OpenPowerlifting is optional, not bundled, and suitable only for row-level strength cohort
  summaries—not longitudinal fat-loss truth or reliable athlete identity linkage.
- Synthetic fixtures have fixed seeds and source labels. They have no external license dependency
  because they are generated by this project.

See [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) and `data/registry/sources.yml` for source-specific records.

## Quality, bias, and leakage checklist

- Verify units, time zone, day boundaries, duplicate-device behavior, and missingness before analysis.
- Never sum overlapping phone/watch daily totals without a declared reconciliation rule.
- Treat logged intake as measurement with potentially systematic—not random—error.
- Do not split repeated rows from one person across train/test sets and call that person-independent
  validation. CutSignal currently trains no population prediction model and ships no such split.
- Do not infer body composition, energy expenditure ground truth, or causality from scale trends.
- Do not use competition weigh-ins as longitudinal body-fat outcomes.
- Validate food labels, allergens, prices, and availability locally before relying on a plan.

## Privacy and retention

Private exports belong under ignored `data/private/` or another non-synced directory. The project has
no account, telemetry, hosted database, or model API. `share-safe` removes high-risk structures but
does not guarantee anonymity. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Fitness for use

Appropriate: local exploration, software demonstrations, reproducible method stress tests, and
human-reviewed decision support inside the declared gates.

Not established: diagnosis, calorie prescription, clinical care, autonomous intervention,
population benchmarking, subgroup fairness, causal effects, or prospective outcome improvement.
